Questions

Frequently asked.

How long does a brand identity project take?

It depends on scope — a naming and identity system takes longer than a refresh. We agree a schedule after the discovery call. If you need to move fast, the two-week Brand Sprint covers strategy, a logo system and a launch kit.

Can you refresh our existing brand instead of starting over?

Yes. Many brands need evolution, not replacement. We audit what already has recognition and keep it, then fix what is holding the brand back.

What files do we receive at the end?

Master logo files in vector and web formats, colour and type specifications, the brand guidelines and editable templates for the launch kit.
